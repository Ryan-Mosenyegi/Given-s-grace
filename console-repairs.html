A REPAIR-FIRST APPROACH

Keep the console.
Fix the problem.

Your console is more than a box of components. It’s your saved progress, time with friends and favourite way to switch off. At Given's Grace, we focus on understanding the fault before recommending a repair.

From power and HDMI faults to overheating and software issues, tell us the model and the symptoms. We’ll advise on the next steps and explain the proposed work before going ahead.

Repair options depend on your model and the fault found during assessment.

COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Won’t switch on

    No power, unexpected shutdowns or a console that refuses to start.

  • No picture or signal

    HDMI connection issues, display faults or a blank screen.

  • Overheating or noisy fans

    Excessive heat, loud cooling or shutdowns during play.

  • Disc and loading problems

    Games that won’t load, read errors or a stuck disc.

  • Controller connection issues

    Pairing problems, unresponsive inputs or charging trouble.

  • Software and performance

    Freezing, failed updates or errors that interrupt your game.
