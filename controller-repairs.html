CONTROLLER SERVICES

Two ways to get more from your controller.

Stick Drift Fix

Stick drift can develop as the stick’s sensor wears with use, or when dust, debris or an impact affects its movement. We inspect and clean the stick first.

If cleaning doesn’t solve the drift, we replace the sticks with standard replacement sticks and check that movement and centering respond properly.

Controller Upgrade

Upgrade your controller with TMR or Hall effect sticks and other parts. Tell us your controller model and what you’d like upgraded.

TMR sensors read changes in magnetic resistance. Hall effect sensors read changes in a magnetic field. Both track movement without sliding electrical contacts, helping reduce wear in the stick sensor.

Why choose TMR?

Many players prefer TMR sticks for their precise input and durable, contactless sensing. Hall effect sticks also use contactless magnetic sensing, avoiding rubbing contacts in the sensor and helping reduce wear over time.

COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Stick drift

    The cursor or camera moves on its own, or a stick feels loose.

  • Sticky or dead buttons

    Buttons that stick, register twice or stop responding.

  • Won’t charge

    The controller loses charge fast or won’t switch on at all.

  • Connection drops

    Pairing fails, the link cuts out or inputs arrive late.

  • Damaged parts

    Cracked shells, worn triggers and bumpers, or a broken charging port.

  • Calibration and updates

    Resets, firmware updates and recalibration when inputs feel off.
