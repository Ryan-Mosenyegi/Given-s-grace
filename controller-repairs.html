COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Stick drift

    The cursor or camera moves on its own, or a stick feels loose.

  • Sticky or dead buttons

    Buttons that stick, register twice or stop responding.

  • Won’t charge

    The controller loses charge fast or won’t switch on at all.

  • Connection drops

    Pairing fails, the link cuts out or inputs arrive late.

  • Damaged parts

    Cracked shells, worn triggers and bumpers, or a broken charging port.

  • Calibration and updates

    Resets, firmware updates and recalibration when inputs feel off.
