A REPAIR-FIRST APPROACH

If it plugs in,
we’ll look.

Not every fault fits a neat category, and that’s fine. If it plugs in, charges or switches on, we can look at what’s going wrong and tell you what a repair would involve before we start.

Describe the device and what it does or doesn’t do. We’ll tell you what we find and what the options are.

Whether a repair is possible depends on the device and the fault found.

COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Won’t charge or start

    The battery doesn’t hold charge, or the device is completely dead.

  • Ports and connectors

    Charging or headphone ports that are loose, bent or full of debris.

  • Internal faults

    Something inside has failed and the cause isn’t obvious from outside.

  • Unknown faults

    You don’t know what’s wrong and want someone to find out.

  • Odd behaviour

    It works, then stops, makes noises or resets by itself.

  • Buttons and switches

    Buttons that stick, switches that don’t click and dials that no longer turn.
