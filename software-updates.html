COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Updates that stall

    An update freezes, loops or stops partway through.

  • Slow performance

    Long loading times, lag and a device that feels slower than it used to.

  • Errors and crashes

    Error codes, apps that close on their own or frequent freezing.

  • Resets and set-up

    A factory reset or fresh set-up when the device needs a clean start.

  • Firmware problems

    An interrupted firmware update that leaves a device unable to start.

  • Stuck on start-up

    The device hangs on the logo or restarts in a loop.
