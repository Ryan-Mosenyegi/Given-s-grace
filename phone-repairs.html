COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Cracked screen

    Cracked glass, dead patches, lines or a display that ignores touch.

  • Battery drain

    The battery empties fast, or the phone switches off with charge left.

  • Charging port

    The cable falls out, charging is slow or nothing happens.

  • Calls and sound

    A muffled speaker, a microphone that cuts out or poor call quality.

  • Gets very hot

    The phone heats up during normal use or while charging.

  • Software trouble

    Freezing, failed updates and apps that keep closing.
