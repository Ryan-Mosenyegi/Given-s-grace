A REPAIR-FIRST APPROACH

Find the fault
before the fix.

A TV can fail in a few different places: the power supply, the screen or the sound. We work out which one is at fault first, then explain the proposed work so you can decide whether to go ahead.

Send us the make, model and what happens when you switch it on. We’ll come back with the likely cause and the next step.

Repair options depend on your TV model and the fault found during assessment.

COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Won’t switch on

    No lights, a standby light that never changes, or a TV that restarts on its own.

  • No picture or lines

    A black screen, flicker, lines or patches on the display.

  • Sound problems

    Crackling or distorted sound, or none at all.

  • Shuts off when warm

    The TV gets hot and switches itself off after a while.

  • Smart features fail

    Apps won’t load, Wi-Fi drops or the menu freezes.

  • Remote and inputs

    The remote does nothing, or an HDMI port isn’t detected.
