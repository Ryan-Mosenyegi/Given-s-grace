A REPAIR-FIRST APPROACH

Find where the
heat comes from.

Heat builds up when airflow is blocked or cooling parts wear out, and it can make a device slow down or switch itself off. We look for the cause first, then explain the proposed work before going ahead.

Tell us the device and when it gets hot, for example while charging or during play. We’ll explain what we find.

What we can do depends on your device and what we find during assessment.

COMMON ISSUES

Sound familiar? We can help.

You don’t need to know the cause. Just tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Loud fans

    Fans that whirr, rattle or run at full speed when nothing is happening.

  • Hot to touch

    A casing or surface that gets uncomfortably warm in normal use.

  • Sudden shutdowns

    The device switches off or restarts once it warms up.

  • Slowdowns under load

    Performance drops after a short time playing or streaming.

  • Worn cooling parts

    Dried thermal paste, a failing fan or a damaged heat sink.

  • Blocked vents

    Dust packed into vents that stops air moving through.
